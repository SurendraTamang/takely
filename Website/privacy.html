Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Takely is built to keep your recordings private. This policy explains the little data that is handled, by whom, and why. The controller is .

Your recordings

Recordings, transcripts, captions, summaries and blurs are made and stored on your Mac. We never receive them. If you turn on sharing, recordings are uploaded only to the storage bucket you configure, with keys kept in your Mac's Keychain.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your name, email, billing address and payment details to process the order, under Paddle's privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details from Paddle so we can deliver and support your license.

License checks

When you activate Takely Pro, the app sends your license key and a generic name for your Mac's model (e.g. "Mac (Mac16,1)") to our license server, and re-checks the key about once a week. We store a one-way hash of your key, the order it came from, and your activations. We don't collect usage analytics or telemetry.

Emails

We email your license key after purchase (sent through our email provider) and reply when you write to us. No marketing emails unless you ask for them.

Updates

The app checks for updates by downloading a public update feed; like any web request, this reveals your IP address to the server hosting it.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or ask a question, by writing to . Deleting your license data ends the license.
