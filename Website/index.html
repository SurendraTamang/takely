Questions

Is anything uploaded?

No. Recording, captions, AI summaries and blurring all run on your Mac. Nothing leaves it unless you set up sharing, and then only to the storage bucket you choose.

How does the license work?

After buying you get a key on screen and by email. Paste it in Takely › Settings › License. It works on up to 3 Macs; deactivate one to move it.
